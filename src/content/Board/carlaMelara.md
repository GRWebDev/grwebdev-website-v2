She is known for turning messy customer needs into clear priorities and helping teams ship software that people love to use. Her 10+ years of business operations give her a practical view of the real workflows and pain points behind the products she builds.

Carla is the Principal Consultant of CM Launch Lab, where she builds AI-powered tools that help small businesses turn customer feedback and everyday processes into practical results. She joined the GRWebDev board to help grow a welcoming community where people from every background can learn, connect, and build in tech.
