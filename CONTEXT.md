# GRWebDev

GRWebDev brings people together to learn, present, and discuss web development in Grand Rapids.

## Language

**Community Code Showcase**:
An informal GRWebDev event where community members share projects, tools, or development discoveries through demos or talks of up to 10 minutes. The 2026 showcase takes place on November 23 at 6 p.m.

**Showcase submission**:
A prospective presenter's proposal to share a project or topic at the Community Code Showcase. Submissions for the 2026 showcase are due November 13.
_Avoid_: Attendee registration, survey response
